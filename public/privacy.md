# Privacy

lenguyenvu.com is a personal website. It has no accounts, no forms, no newsletter and no advertising. It sets no cookies and stores nothing in your browser.

## What is processed when you visit

- The site is hosted on Cloudflare. Like any web host, Cloudflare processes request data such as your IP address and browser user agent to deliver pages and protect the site from abuse.
- Cloudflare Web Analytics is enabled on the domain. It counts page views without cookies and without tracking you across other sites.
- Photos, films and PDFs are served from media.lenguyenvu.com, also on Cloudflare.
- Fonts load from Google Fonts, so your browser sends a request to Google when the page loads.

## Links and AI agents

Links to other sites, such as LinkedIn, X, GitHub and event pages, follow those sites' own privacy policies. AI search engines and assistants may read and cite this site's public pages; crawlers that train AI models are asked not to via robots.txt.
