# Product Judgment

How to make product decisions for the portfolio.

## The product

The portfolio is a personal marketing surface for Louis Le, Co-Founder & CTO
of cf0.ai. The audience is: recruiters, founders, investors, collaborators,
and peers in AI/finance.

## The job

The user (visitor) is evaluating Louis for:
- A role (internship, contract, full-time)
- An investment (cf0.ai)
- A collaboration (hackathon, open source, research)
- A reference check (is this person credible?)

## Primary action

The primary action is **contact**: email louis@cf0.ai. The secondary action
is **explore work**: scroll through capabilities, projects, research.

## Content principles

- **Ground everything in the CV.** The CV (`docs/me.md` and the source
  `.tex`) is the source of truth for bio, experience, projects, research,
  and skills. Do not invent claims.
- **Lead with the current role.** Co-Founder & CTO at cf0.ai is the lead
  identity. Not "student" or "intern."
- **Show, don't tell.** "1st Place, Lovable Hackathon" is evidence. "Award
 -winning builder" is not.
- **Quantify where possible.** "82% AML classification" is evidence. "High
  accuracy" is not.
- **No marketing language.** No "cutting-edge," "world-class," "passionate
  ," "leverage," "synergy." See `copy.md`.

## Decision framework

When deciding what to add, change, or remove:

1. **Does it serve the primary job?** If not, cut it.
2. **Is it grounded in the CV?** If not, cut it or add it to the CV first.
3. **Is it the smallest coherent change?** If not, scope it down.
4. **Does it preserve the editorial aesthetic?** If it adds decoration
   without aiding comprehension, cut it.

## What the portfolio is NOT

- Not a blog.
- Not a dashboard.
- Not a demo of animation libraries.
- Not a place for decorative effects (Dither, TargetCursor, TextPressure,
  FlowingMenu, D3 graphs, IconCloud — all removed).
- Not a dark-mode site. Light-mode only, editorial canvas.
