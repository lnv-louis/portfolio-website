# Copy

User-facing language standards for the portfolio.

## Voice

Louis Le's voice: direct, technical, no fluff. Receipts before claims.
Concrete numbers, not vibes. Short sentences. No hedging.

## Rules

### rule/no-marketing-language
Source: portfolio voice
Rule: No marketing language. No "cutting-edge," "world-class," "passionate
," "leverage," "synergy," "innovative," "disruptive," "game-changing."
Why: Marketing language reads as empty. Evidence reads as credible.
Exceptions: None.
Bad: "Award-winning builder passionate about cutting-edge AI"
Good: "1st Place, Lovable Hackathon. Co-Founder & CTO, cf0.ai."

### rule/canonical-product-names
Source: glossary.md
Rule: Use canonical names: cf0.ai (not CF0, Cf0, cf0 AI), UCL (not
University College London unless first mention), The Hack Collective (not
Hack Collective).
Why: Consistent names build recognition.
Exceptions: First mention in a paragraph may expand an acronym.

### rule/verb-noun-for-actions
Source: portfolio voice
Rule: CTAs use verb + noun: "Get in touch," "Read manuscript," "View
project." Never "Click here," "Learn more," "Submit."
Why: Verb + noun tells the user what happens. Generic labels don't.
Exceptions: None.
Bad: "Click here", "Learn more", "Submit"
Good: "Get in touch", "Read manuscript", "View project"

## Eyebrows

Eyebrows are uppercase, mono, 12px, weight 500, tracking 0.12em. They label
sections: `WHAT I DO`, `SELECTED WORK`, `RESEARCH`, `CONTACT`.

## Headings

Headings use Source Serif 4, sentence case, weight 500. They are
declarative, not interrogative: "Projects that shipped." not "Check out my
projects!"

## Body copy

Body copy uses Inter, sentence case, weight 400. Short paragraphs. No
em-dashes in Louis's own prose (commas and periods carry connectives).
